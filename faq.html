Questions, Answered

Everything students ask before joining, in plain English. Can't find yours? Email thinker@philoinvestor.com.

Enrollment
How do I join Philo's Academy?+
Click any "Start Learning" button on the site. Checkout runs through Whop. Once you're through, you get access to every module, case study, and workshop.
Is there a free trial?+
There isn't a formal trial, but you don't have to take our word for it first. There's 6+ hours of free teaching on YouTube, including the first 10 minutes of every module, so you can see the teaching style before you enroll.
What's your refund policy?+
Refund requests are considered case-by-case. Contact us within 14 days of purchase if you'd like to request one. See our Terms of Service for the full policy.
Can I share my account with someone else?+
No. Enrollment is for your personal use only. Sharing your login or redistributing course materials isn't allowed and may result in your access being revoked without refund.
The Course
What's actually inside the course?+
Four parts: Foundational Concepts & Value Investing, Advanced Investment Concepts, Mastery & Strategy, and Managing Risk & Portfolio Management, taught through modules, case studies applying the frameworks to real companies, and live workshops with student Q&A. See everything inside.
Is it self-paced?+
Yes. Modules, case studies, and workshop recordings are all available on demand, so you can work through the four parts at your own pace.
Do I need any prior experience?+
No. Part 1 builds your foundation from scratch, and the course is built for a wide range of starting points. See who joins Philo's Academy.
Are results guaranteed?+
No. Investing involves risk, including the possible loss of principal, and past performance isn't indicative of future results. See our Disclaimer for the full statement.
Is This For You
I'm just starting out. Is this too advanced for me?+
Not at all. Part 1 is built for beginners building their foundation, and the course walks up from there. You don't need any background going in.
I'm already an experienced investor. Will I learn anything new?+
Parts 2 through 4 cover professional-level frameworks: analyst-grade valuation, strategy and macro integration, and portfolio risk management. It's built for beginners and professionals re-discovering their edge alike.
I mostly invest in ETFs. Is this still relevant to me?+
Yes. Moving from passive ETF investing into confident stock-picking is one of the most common paths students take through the course.
Corporate & Teams
Do you offer packages for teams?+
Yes. We offer education packages for in-house investment teams, family offices, and advisory firms. Inquire about corporate training.
Who teaches the course?+
Philo, from Philoinvestor: 20+ years investing experience, followed by 20,000+ people on X & Substack.
