The Result

PART 1 — Foundational Concepts & Value Investing

Build an Unshakable Base

  • Explain drivers of business value & returns in plain English.
  • Spot traps early (story stocks, fake cheap, narrative FOMO).
  • Define your circle of competence and operate with rules.
  • Shift from headline-reactive to calm, repeatable process.

PART 2 — Advanced Investment Concepts

Think Like a Professional Analyst

  • Break down any business model and value it correctly.
  • Identify optionality & disruption before consensus.
  • Source ideas systematically; kill value traps fast.
  • Map ranges of outcomes and size positions accordingly.

PART 3 — Mastery & Strategy

Codify Your Edge

  • Use high-efficiency frameworks to decide faster, better.
  • Integrate macro context without drifting into tourism.
  • Systematize adds/trims, catalysts, & review loops.
  • Operate simple, systematic, decisive.

PART 4 — Managing Risk & Portfolio Management

Portfolio Mastery & Resilience

  • Build a portfolio that survives disruption & uncertainty.
  • Apply real risk rules: sizing, liquidity, reflexivity, cycles.
  • Stress-test: pre-mortems, exit rules, error budgets.
  • Leave with your own decision operating system.
